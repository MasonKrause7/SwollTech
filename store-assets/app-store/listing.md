## Description (4000)
SwollTech is a workout log for lifters who run their own training. You plan the program and pick the progressions. SwollTech makes every set fast to record and your progress easy to see.

LOG FASTER
• Each set opens with what you did last time. Adjust it with a tap and move on.
• Warm-up sets, RPE, and notes, only when you want them.
• Built-in rest timer that keeps running while you check your phone.
• Plate math for every barbell weight, in pounds or kilograms.
• Works offline. Log in a basement gym with no signal, and it syncs when you're back online.

SEE YOUR PROGRESS
• Estimated one-rep max and top-set trends for every lift, across your full history.
• Personal records the moment you set them.
• Weekly volume and working sets, plus streaks that show your consistency.
• A summary after every workout, with volume, sets, time, and records.

PLAN YOUR WEEK
• Build workouts once and reuse them.
• Schedule them on the calendar and see what's next.

STAYS OUT OF THE WAY
• No social feed, no ads, no gamified noise.
• Free means free: unlimited workouts and exercises, with charts that don't stop at three months.
• Use it on your phone or at swolltech.com. Your log stays in sync.

YOUR DATA
• Your training data is yours. We don't sell it.
• Delete your account and all of your data at any time, right in the app.
